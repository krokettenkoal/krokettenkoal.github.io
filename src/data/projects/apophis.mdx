## Overview

Imagine a world suspended in the vast void of space, shaped by surreal organic forms and shimmering crystalline structures.
In Apophis, players don’t just explore this mysterious landscape—they unravel a tale of discovery, survival, and innovation.
This VR adventure combines exploration, resource management, and immersive storytelling to create an experience like no other.

<CarouselImages client:visible images={carouselOverview} />

At the heart of Apophis lies the desire to create a fresh and captivating VR experience.
Players take on the role of a scientist piloting a versatile vehicle designed for exploration and resource collection.
_Their journey?_ To traverse an alien world filled with obstacles, puzzles, and secrets, all while facing a looming threat embodied by a mischievous off-screen antagonist.

## Gameplay
Designed for the Meta Quest 3 VR headset, Apophis prioritizes simple, intuitive gameplay to ensure accessibility for new VR players while still captivating seasoned explorers.
The single-player experience offers a smooth and immersive world that encourages players to advance at their own pace.

<CarouselImages client:visible images={carouselGameplay} />

The gameplay in Apophis revolves around three primary elements:
1. **Exploration:** Navigate four distinct areas, each presenting unique environments, dangers, and opportunities.
2. **Resource Management:** Destroy certain parts of the world to gather resources and unlock new paths. Players can use harvested materials to replicate key items, progressing further into the game.
3. **Obstacle Navigation:** Utilize the vehicle’s various modes, including a gravity gun, laser, and flamethrower, to overcome different challenges.

Each obstacle requires players to think critically and leverage their resources wisely.
Tutorials guide players when encountering new mechanics, ensuring a seamless and engaging learning curve.

## Development

Apophis was developed as part of the master class _Augmented & Virtual Reality_ within the _Interactive Technologies_ master program at UAS St. Pöten during the 2024 summer and winter semesters.
The game was developed in Unity using C#. Audio was implemented using the Wwise middleware. The 3D team used a combination of Maya and Blender for creating our assets. Furthermore, Notion was used for project management.
