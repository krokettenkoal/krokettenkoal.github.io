> From table to kitchen, in just a tap.

Orderbar is a digital menu with order functionality as well as a management view for staff.
Customers can browse the menu, add items to their order and submit it directly to the kitchen, while staff can view all tables, their orders and manage them accordingly.
For demonstration purposes, the final application was built for the fictional restaurant *Hephaistos*, but can easily be adapted for any restaurant, bar or other business with table service.
