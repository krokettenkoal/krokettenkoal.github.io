**Spooked!** is a short, interactive visual novel that combines a playful supernatural setting with branching dialogue and multiple endings.
Players join Skully, a friendly skeleton, on a quest to find Batty, a sleepy bat, and decide how their little adventure unfolds.
With character interactions, atmospheric backgrounds, animated sprites, and a spooky audiovisual presentation, the game brings its quirky cast to life.

Different choices lead to different outcomes, encouraging players to explore the story and discover its endings.
Developed as a demo project for the university course *2D Game Animation and Interactive Storytelling*, Spooked! also serves as a practical introduction to the fundamentals of visual novel development using Ren'Py.

## Development

**Spooked!** was developed using Ren'Py and its Python-based scripting language, demonstrating core concepts of visual novel development through a compact, playable example.

The project showcases several key features:

- **Branching narrative:** Labels, conditional logic, menus, and jumps structure the story and connect player choices to different narrative outcomes.
- **Character and state management:** Character definitions, dialogue interpolation, and variables track the player's chosen name and story progress, influencing subsequent scenes.
- **2D animation and transforms:** Animated WebM character sprites, layered character states, custom positioning transforms, movement transitions, and a procedural shaking animation bring the characters to life.
- **Video and scene transitions:** A prerendered introductory cutscene, animated backgrounds, and transitions between scenes establish the game's atmosphere and pacing.
- **Audio implementation:** Background music, ambient sound, sound effects, volume control, fade transitions, and a low-pass audio filter reinforce the mood of individual scenes and endings.
- **Custom presentation:** A custom display font, reusable text styling, and Ren'Py's image and screen presentation features support the game's visual identity.
- **Multiple endings:** Separate ending labels demonstrate how narrative branches can lead to distinct outcomes and how audiovisual elements can be adapted to each conclusion.

The project was designed as both a standalone narrative experience and an educational resource, illustrating how Ren'Py combines scripting, visual assets, animation, audio, and interactive storytelling within an accessible development workflow.
