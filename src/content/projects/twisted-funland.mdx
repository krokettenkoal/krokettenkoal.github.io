## Development

**Twisted Funland was developed as a solo project in Unity**, with all gameplay implementation and technical integration handled independently.
The project combines VR interaction, environmental puzzles, skill-based minigames, and spatial audio to create an immersive escape room experience set in a twisted amusement park.

### VR Development & Interaction

Built using Unity and the XR Interaction Toolkit, the project features interactive challenges designed around exploration, object interaction, and player-driven problem-solving.
From solving environmental riddles to completing carnival minigames, the gameplay brings together a variety of mechanics within a cohesive VR experience.

### Audio Integration & Immersion

Audio plays an essential role in establishing the park's eerie atmosphere and guiding players through their journey.
Wwise was integrated alongside the Meta XR Audio SDK to support the project's audio implementation and VR sound experience.

The voice-over was AI-generated using ElevenLabs, providing narrative guidance, contextual hints, and feedback as players progress through the challenges.

### Gameplay & Level Implementation

The experience connects multiple themed areas through a sequence of puzzles, minigames, and progression objectives.
Players must discover clues, complete challenges, and find essential items to unlock new opportunities and ultimately escape the park.
All gameplay systems and project implementation were developed by me, bringing the individual interactions and objectives together into a complete VR escape room.

### Assets & Production

Third-party models, textures, and sound recordings were used to build the amusement park environment and establish its distinctive atmosphere.
These assets were integrated into the Unity project as part of the overall level, gameplay, and audio implementation.

### My Role

As the sole developer, I was responsible for all implementation and technical integration of the project, including VR interaction, gameplay mechanics, puzzle and minigame implementation, and audio integration.
This involved bringing the different components together into a cohesive, interactive VR experience.
