> Discovering tomorrow what we lost today.

Notes from the Past explores human culture through the lens of an **alien lifeform**.
Discovering the _Voyager_ probe and deciphering the **Golden Record** led these aliens to find music.
Enthralled by melodies never thought possible, they began their **journey to Earth**.
While exploring an abandoned house and its surroundings, an alien discovers the story of the family that lived there and of **humanity’s sudden end.**

Explore an expansive house filled with puzzles. Collect artifacts of humanity and find out what caused civilization to end.
Listen to recordings of the people that lived there and learn about their fate.

## Development

Notes from the Past was developed during the MediaLab _Digital Game Production_ within the _Media Technology_ bachelor program at University of Applied Sciences St. Pölten during the 2022 winter semester.
The game was developed in Unity, using Audiokinetic Wwise as audio middleware.
