## Mission Objectives

In order to gain access to the main vault, you need to find the according keycard in the map.
There are three different types of keycards:
- Employee Keycard
- CEO Keycard
- Security Keycard

The keycards spawn on random positions in the world and unlock various numbers of doors according to their security level.
Having acquired all necessary keycards, your next goal is to find and open the main vault.
Grab as much cash and valuables you can and escape by using one of the getaway opportunities before time runs off.

Additionally to the main objectives, you can also try to gain access to the security room and disable all CCTV cameras.
This gives you a time bonus and makes moving inside the building easier.

## Code-locked doors

As soon as the alarm is triggered, all doors are being closed and locked by a random 4-digit numeric code.
To unlock a code-locked door, type in the highlighted digits in ascending order in the keypad.

## Development

Jesus Heist, Superstar was developed as a solo-project for the _Digital Game Production_ elective module within the _Media Technology_ bachelor program at University of Applied Sciences St. Pölten during the 2022 summer semester.
The game was developed in Unity, using Audiokinetic Wwise as audio middleware.

### My Role

Since this was a solo-project, I was responsible for all aspects of the game development, including development, level design, and audio implementation.
